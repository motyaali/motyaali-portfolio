How the work connects

A larger system developed piece by piece.

The projects were not created in a tidy linear sequence. They emerged as different responses to recurring problems: fragmented information, lost context, inaccessible processes, decision overload, and the gap between analysis and execution.

The through-line

Clarity, evidence, practicality, accessibility, and human control.

My goal is not to automate judgment away. It is to build systems that preserve context, surface uncertainty, reduce avoidable friction, and help people make better decisions.
