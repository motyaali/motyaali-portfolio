Meeting Intelligence Review Prototype

Turn meeting material into proposed decisions, actions, owners, dates, risks, dependencies, questions, and controls, then require accountable review before an operating record is generated.

Prototype boundary: This browser-local demonstration uses a fictional meeting and deterministic pre-generated proposals. It demonstrates source-preserving review, correction, approval, rejection, and evidence export. It does not claim production AI accuracy, live system integration, or autonomous authority.

1. Review the synthetic source meeting

The source remains visible so each proposed record can be verified against what was actually said.

2. Inspect and correct proposed records

Use detailed review for ambiguity, missing information, high-impact items, and records that may become authoritative.

No proposals loaded.

3. Generate the reviewed operating record

Only approved records appear in the approved section. Edits, rejected items, and pending proposals remain visible in the evidence history.

The reviewed record will appear here.

Where this control pattern belongs

Use detailed review for

  • Ambiguous or incomplete records
  • High-impact proposed actions
  • Records that may become authoritative
  • Exceptions requiring accountable judgment
  • Quality sampling and model evaluation

Use lighter controls for

  • Low-risk routine records
  • Fields established by deterministic rules
  • Duplicate mechanical checks
  • Reversible preparation work
  • Work where detailed review would cost more than the risk it controls
