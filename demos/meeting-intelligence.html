Proposal Review and Audit Control Prototype

This prototype isolates one reusable governance component: how proposed records can be checked, corrected, rejected, approved, and retained with a visible decision history.

Correct role: This is not the customer-facing MVP for AI Workflow Enablement. It is supporting technical evidence for workflows where proposed records require human review. The primary value demonstration is the Document Intake and Routing demonstration.

1. Synthetic source meeting

The source remains visible so a reviewer can verify each proposed record.

2. Inspect proposed records

This deliberately detailed view is appropriate for uncertain or high-impact records, not routine low-risk processing.

No proposals loaded.

3. Generate the reviewed evidence record

Only approved records appear in the published output. Rejected items and edits remain in the exported review history.

The reviewed record will appear here.

Where this control pattern belongs

Use detailed review for

  • Ambiguous or incomplete records
  • High-impact proposed actions
  • Records that will become authoritative
  • Exceptions that require accountable judgment
  • Quality sampling and model evaluation

Do not require it for

  • Every low-risk routine record
  • Fields already established by deterministic rules
  • Duplicate mechanical checks
  • Repeated ceremonial approvals
  • Work where the review costs more than the risk
