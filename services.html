Proportional control

The review process should cost less than the risk it controls.

Not every record deserves the same approval burden. The workflow separates routine, reversible, rule-based work from uncertain, incomplete, high-impact, or policy-sensitive exceptions.

Routine path

  • Deterministic checks and required-field validation
  • Prepared names, metadata, destinations, or draft records
  • Batch confirmation, sampling, or monitored auto-processing where appropriate

Exception path

  • Duplicates and conflicting information
  • Missing required identifiers or permissions
  • Low-confidence classification or extraction
  • Irreversible, authoritative, or high-impact actions
  • Policy, legal, safety, eligibility, or financial judgment

The pilot tests whether the chosen control points save work in practice rather than assuming more approvals are always safer.

Controlled service pilot

Bring one recurring process. Leave with measured evidence.

The minimum viable business is a bounded implementation, not a standalone SaaS product: one process, one department, one approved source environment, and a defined group of users.

Included

  • Discovery, current-state map, and baseline effort
  • Future-state workflow and proportional review rules
  • Configured demonstration or pilot
  • Representative routine records, exceptions, and failure tests
  • Acceptance criteria and correction evidence
  • User guide, administrator runbook, and training
  • Documented recommendation to scale, revise, or stop

Good pilot fit

  • Repeated meetings, documents, requests, reports, approvals, or knowledge questions
  • A named process owner
  • Approved access to representative data
  • Willingness to test ambiguity, duplicates, failures, and permissions
  • Agreement to measure both saved work and review burden

Not offered

No autonomous legal, medical, financial, benefits, hiring, safety, or eligibility determinations. No production use of sensitive data before authorization and controls are documented.
