Proportional control

The review process should cost less than the risk it controls.

Not every record needs the same approval burden. The workflow separates routine, reversible, rule-based preparation from uncertain, incomplete, high-impact, or policy-sensitive exceptions.

Routine path

  • Required-field and deterministic checks
  • Prepared names, metadata, destinations, summaries, or draft records
  • Batch confirmation, sampling, or monitored processing where appropriate

Exception path

  • Duplicates and conflicting information
  • Missing identifiers, records, or permissions
  • Low-confidence classification or extraction
  • Irreversible, authoritative, or high-impact actions
  • Policy, legal, safety, eligibility, financial, or contractual judgment

The pilot tests whether the selected controls reduce work in practice while preserving accountability.

Controlled service pilot

Bring one recurring process. Leave with evidence and a decision about whether to scale it.

A pilot is bounded to one recurring workflow, one process owner, one team or business unit, an approved environment, representative approved data, and a defined user group. The objective is to determine whether the future-state workflow reduces enough administrative burden to justify adoption and continued support.

Included

  • Discovery, current-state map, and baseline effort
  • Future-state workflow and review rules
  • Configured demonstration or controlled pilot
  • Representative routine records, exceptions, and failure tests
  • Acceptance criteria and correction evidence
  • User guide, administrator runbook, and training
  • Recommendation to scale, revise, or stop

Recommended boundary

  • One recurring process with a clear owner and measurable pain
  • One team or business unit
  • Approximately 5 to 20 pilot users
  • Representative, approved data limited to the defined process
  • Systems the organization already owns whenever practical
  • Success criteria covering accuracy, safe failure, adoption, cycle time, correction rate, and client acceptance

Not offered

No autonomous legal, medical, financial, benefits, hiring, safety, eligibility, payment, contract-approval, or policy-exception determinations. No production use of sensitive data before authorization and controls are documented.
