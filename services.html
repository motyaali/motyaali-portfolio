Controlled pilot

Bring one recurring process. Leave with evidence.

The first engagement is intentionally narrow: one process, one department, one approved source environment, and a defined group of users.

Included

  • Discovery and current-state map
  • Future-state workflow and governance rules
  • Configured demonstration or pilot
  • Representative test records and acceptance criteria
  • Human-review and exception handling
  • User guide, administrator runbook, and training
  • Documented recommendation to scale, revise, or stop

Good pilot fit

  • Repeated meetings, documents, requests, reports, approvals, or knowledge questions
  • A named process owner
  • Approved access to representative data
  • Willingness to test ambiguity, duplicates, failures, and permissions
  • Human review where judgment matters

Not offered

No autonomous legal, medical, financial, benefits, hiring, safety, or eligibility determinations. No production use of sensitive data before authorization and controls are documented.
