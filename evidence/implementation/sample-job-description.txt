Operations Coordinator | Example Community Organization

Responsibilities
- Maintain accurate records and scheduling information.
- Track actions, owners, deadlines, and unresolved requests.
- Explain operating procedures to staff and volunteers.
- Use spreadsheets to prepare weekly status updates.

Interview focus
Ask for a specific example of resolving a scheduling conflict, documenting a process, and following up on an overdue action. Explore the candidate's contribution without inventing metrics or experience.
