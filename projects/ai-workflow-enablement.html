The operational problem

Meeting knowledge is often trapped in notes, memory, and disconnected follow-up.

Teams lose decisions, actions, owners, due dates, risks, and unresolved questions when meeting material is not converted into a governed operating record. A transcript summary alone does not solve the problem because it may still lack accountability, review, source traceability, and maintenance ownership.

MVP success criteria

  • Preserve the synthetic source material
  • Generate structured proposals rather than final decisions
  • Require explicit approval or rejection
  • Keep rejected and edited items visible in the review history
  • Export a reviewed operating record
  • Disclose limitations and production dependencies

Architecture

A proposal-to-approval workflow.

The public MVP uses deterministic synthetic proposals so reviewers can inspect the governance and operating design without sending data to an external model. A production pilot would replace that proposal generator with an approved model or workplace connector.

01

Capture

Receive approved notes or a transcript and preserve the source.

02

Propose

Identify candidate decisions, actions, owners, dates, risks, and questions.

03

Review

A person approves, edits, or rejects every proposed record.

04

Publish

Generate the reviewed record and retain the decision history.

05

Measure

Track corrections, unresolved ownership, failures, and improvement work.
