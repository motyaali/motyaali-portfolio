The continuity problem

One missing connection can create a chain of missed obligations.

Important information is often distributed across files, messages, dates, people, agencies, systems, and unfinished tasks. A notice may mention a deadline, require evidence stored elsewhere, depend on a person who has not replied, and create a follow-up that is easy to lose after the immediate crisis passes.

Unseen OS turns those disconnected records into linked entities and open loops so the user can see what matters now, what remains unresolved, and what evidence proves completion.

Notice Response Mode

A focused path for urgent institutional information.

The mode applies the same source-first pattern used in Unseen Lifeline while preserving the resulting records inside the continuity system.

01

Ingest

Store the notice and record source, issuer, date, and stated consequence.

02

Extract

Prepare requirements, dates, evidence needs, contacts, and unclear terms.

03

Connect

Link existing documents, people, prior communications, and related open loops.

04

Review

Verify facts, correct uncertainty, approve drafts, and choose the response path.

05

Preserve

Track submission, confirmation, promised follow-up, and unresolved obligations.

Selected design decisions

Continuity requires more than extracting dates.

  • Keep every derived item linked to the source record.
  • Distinguish a deadline stated in a document from one calculated by the system.
  • Track awaited replies and missing evidence as first-class open loops.
  • Record completion evidence, not only task status.
  • Use weekly review to surface stale and blocked work after the immediate crisis passes.
  • Keep consequential communications and submissions under human review.
