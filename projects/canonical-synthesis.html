Operational System

Canonical Source Synthesis System

A controlled information-governance method for converting fragmented files and conflicting source material into approved references and repeatable operating outputs.

Overview

The system was designed to reduce the cognitive and operational burden of comparing many versions of documents while preserving uncertainty, evidence, and human authority.

Challenge

When information exists across duplicate files, partial records, conflicting drafts, and multiple tools, people can spend more time locating and reconciling sources than making decisions.

My role

System design, governance rules, review-matrix design, prompt and workflow architecture, documentation, testing through real information-management tasks, and final human approval.

What I created

  • Source inventory and document-family clustering
  • Evidence-linked recommendation tables
  • Conflict and unique-detail maps
  • Reversible review queues and approval gates
  • Role-specific output packets
  • Operator documentation and change controls

Outcome and current maturity

The result is a repeatable system in which agents may inventory, compare, recommend, stage, and draft, while canonical decisions remain human-only.

Evidence treatment

This public page uses approved descriptions and, where needed, sanitized or reconstructed evidence. Private archives, employer-confidential material, personal records, credentials, and raw source files are not published.
