Operating method

How I approach planning work

  1. Establish the financial, inventory, margin, and service objectives.
  2. Separate demand change from timing, distribution, pricing, and data-quality effects.
  3. Quantify the month, quarter, season, and year impact.
  4. Identify controllable levers, constraints, and decision deadlines.
  5. Translate the analysis into clear actions, owners, and follow-up measures.
