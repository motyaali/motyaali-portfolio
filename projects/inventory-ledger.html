Documented Architecture

Inventory Truth Ledger

An enterprise retail architecture for reconciling inventory signals across planning, ERP, warehouse, transportation, purchase-order, receiving, quality, and carrier systems.

Overview

The project applies deep retail-planning and inventory experience to a recurring enterprise problem: several systems can each contain a partial or differently timed version of inventory truth.

Challenge

Planning, ERP, warehouse, transportation, receiving, quality, and carrier systems may report different quantities or states. Without an explicit reconciliation model, teams debate numbers rather than resolve causes.

My role

Problem framing, domain model design, source and state mapping, exception logic, workflow architecture, and documentation of a proposed decision-support system.

What I created

  • Inventory-state and source-system model
  • Reconciliation and exception framework
  • Lineage and freshness concepts
  • Role-specific decision views
  • Proposed audit and escalation logic
  • Architecture and implementation documentation

Outcome and current maturity

A substantial architecture and operating concept exists. A production implementation, live integrations, and validated enterprise results are not claimed.
