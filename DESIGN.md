# Portfolio design direction

This document records the existing, reviewed direction of the portfolio, rather than proposing a new brand.

## Purpose and audience

Present a credible professional record to recruiters, hiring managers, and collaborators assessing business systems, implementation, project operations, retail planning, and applied AI work. Let visitors move quickly from a claim to the case, demo, method, or public-safe evidence supporting it.

## Existing visual direction

- Editorial case-file / operating-dossier presentation, not a SaaS landing-page template.
- Warm paper, charcoal, restrained rust accents. Avoid gradients, floating decorative orbs, glassmorphism, fake terminals, and generic AI iconography.
- Serif headings, readable sans-serif body text, and restrained monospaced labels for evidence and document metadata.
- Clear hierarchy, asymmetric or split feature compositions where they serve the work, compact functional diagrams, and deliberate whitespace.
- Preserve the existing navigation and case-study structure unless there is a demonstrated usability problem.

## Writing and evidence

- Lead with the actual responsibility, implementation, or working behavior, not hype about innovation.
- Preserve the user's professional history. Do not invent a simplistic career-pivot narrative.
- Distinguish (1) firsthand professional experience, (2) public-safe reconstructed cases, (3) browser demos using synthetic inputs, and (4) separately deployed working applications.
- Do not publish confidential employer, client, student, legal, medical, or financial information. Do not manufacture performance metrics, testimonials, credentials, or employer endorsements.
- Prefer specific labels and short, direct copy. Avoid em dashes in new text.

## Usability

- Desktop and mobile should both feel intentionally composed. Content and controls must not clip or overflow.
- Keyboard focus must be obvious. Navigation and links should have real destinations. Preserve reduced-motion behavior.
- Evidence should be inspectable, but visitors should not need to decode internal project jargon to find it.

## Source of this direction

This reflects the existing implementation in `assets/styles.css` and `assets/editorial.css`, the editorial redesign already merged in PR #17, and the user's subsequent portfolio review decisions. Any material change to this direction needs a separate owner review.
