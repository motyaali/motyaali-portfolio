## Project objective

Remove deteriorated structural street gratings and associated supports, repair designated substrate conditions, install replacement assemblies, maintain safe pedestrian access, and complete turnover without disrupting transit service.

## Scope included

- Review and coordination of demolition and replacement requirements
- Temporary barriers, pedestrian routing, dust and noise controls
- Saw-cutting and removal at designated limits
- Removal of identified grating components, saddles, plates, bars, collars, and anchors
- Protection of adjacent structures and active utilities
- Contractor quotations and supporting documentation for potential changes
- Project meeting, action, decision, invoice, and closeout records
- Turnover documentation, warranties, as-built information, and final reconciliation

## Scope excluded or separately authorized

- Hazardous-material abatement not identified in the baseline documents
- Utility relocation unless separately directed
- Design authority or technical approval by the Project Coordinator
- Independent approval of contracts, change orders, invoices, or payments
- Work outside the approved limits without written direction

## Operating rules demonstrated

- Learn and follow the approved procedure before suggesting changes.
- Maintain one visible owner, status, next action, and due date for each open item.
- Preserve source references and revision history.
- Separate administrative completeness from technical, contractual, and financial approval.
- Flag missing support before routing.
- Escalate exceptions with facts, impact, and a requested decision.
- Maintain closeout requirements throughout the project rather than waiting until the end.

## Four control lenses

- **Time:** milestones, dependencies, overdue actions, schedule effects, and decision dates
- **Money:** commitments, quotations, approved changes, invoices, retainage, and reconciliation
- **Safety:** required plans, pedestrian controls, work-hour constraints, and missing approvals
- **Quality:** revision control, technical source references, completeness checks, and turnover records
