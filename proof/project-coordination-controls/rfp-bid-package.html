1. Invitation to bid

Demolition subcontractor procurement

MetroLink Transit Authority invites qualified demolition contractors to submit proposals for the demolition portion of the Urban Transit Station Street Grating Rehabilitation project in the Central Station District of Bay City, California.

The work includes controlled removal of designated pavement, structural grating components, associated supports, and anchors within an active urban transit environment. The successful bidder must maintain safe pedestrian access, protect adjacent structures and utilities, coordinate required notices and submittals, control dust and noise, legally remove debris, and complete the work without interrupting transit operations.

This package defines the requested scope, exclusions, submission requirements, schedule, and evaluation criteria so bidders price the same administrative and technical boundaries. Any exception, qualification, or assumed exclusion must be stated in the proposal.

4. Scope definition

Description of scope of work

The demolition subcontractor shall furnish the labor, supervision, tools, equipment, temporary controls, transportation, disposal, documentation, and incidental materials required to complete the following work within the indicated limits:

  1. Preconstruction planning: Review the drawings and specifications, verify visible conditions, and submit a demolition plan describing sequence, methods, equipment, pedestrian controls, utility protection, salvage, disposal, and emergency contacts. Work may not begin until the required reviewers provide written acceptance.
  2. Permits and notices: Obtain permits and licenses assigned to the subcontractor, coordinate hauling and disposal requirements, provide required notices, and confirm utility-marking procedures before saw-cutting or excavation.
  3. Temporary protection: Install and maintain fencing, barriers, barricades, warning signs, lighting, temporary bracing, and other protections required for active demolition. Maintain safe access and protect transit operations.
  4. Pedestrian and traffic coordination: Submit the required routing and control plan. Phase work to maintain safe pedestrian passage unless authorized written direction states otherwise. Provide flaggers and controlled material-hauling access when required.
  5. Environmental controls: Provide dust suppression, noise control, and vibration monitoring appropriate to the work and approved work hours. Maintain a clean and orderly work area.
  6. Saw-cutting and removals: Saw-cut full depth at designated limits. Remove pavement, grating assemblies, concrete anchors, steel saddles, stiffener plates, collars, vertical and horizontal bars, and other components shown within the demolition limits.
  7. Anchor treatment: Cut anchors flush where permitted. Remove anchors that conflict with new work. Fill abandoned or removal holes with the specified approved grout system.
  8. Utility coordination: Protect known utilities in place. Disconnect, cut, cap, or plug only those services specifically identified for demolition and only after coordination with the utility owner and authorized project personnel.
  9. Backfill and repair: Backfill and compact demolition voids as required by the referenced earthwork requirements. Repair finishes and coatings damaged by demolition operations.
  10. Salvage and disposal: Protect items identified for salvage. Remove waste promptly, use approved recycling or disposal facilities, provide haul tickets, and do not bury or burn debris.
  11. Final cleanup: Remove temporary demolition controls when authorized, clear debris, submit required disposal records, and leave the work area ready for the following trade.

5. Boundary control

Items not in scope

The demolition bid excludes the following unless added by written direction or amendment:

  • Hazardous-material investigation, abatement, transport, or disposal
  • Seismic retrofit work or demolition solely for seismic upgrades
  • Vegetation or tree clearing assigned to a separate specification section
  • Utility relocation beyond identified cut, cap, or protection activities
  • Deep foundation removal beyond the indicated demolition limits
  • Non-structural interior demolition, finishes, furnishings, and unrelated MEP removal
  • Fabrication and installation of replacement grating assemblies
  • Design, engineering, or approval authority
  • Vehicle detours not shown in an approved traffic-control plan
  • Work outside the written limits or after-hours work not separately authorized
Scope clarification rule: A bidder may not convert an unclear requirement into an unlisted exclusion. Questions must be submitted before the RFI deadline, and proposal exceptions must be explicit.

9. Transparency

Reconstruction assumptions and limitations

  • This is a portfolio artifact, not the original class document and not a real solicitation.
  • The structure follows the coursework sequence: define needs, invite bidders, describe requirements, explain the response format, establish weighted criteria, set the timeline, and review the package for consistency.
  • The demolition scope reflects recurring source elements such as saw-cutting, structural removals, anchor treatment, utility protection, dust and noise controls, off-site disposal, submittals, and approved sequencing.
  • Conflicting draft language was not copied into the final scope. The synthetic baseline controls, and every inclusion and exclusion is stated in one place.
  • No claim is made that this package satisfies a specific public agency's current procurement, legal, labor, insurance, environmental, or contracting requirements.
