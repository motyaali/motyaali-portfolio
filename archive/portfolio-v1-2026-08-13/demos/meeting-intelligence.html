Working coordination demonstration | Synthetic data

From scattered team updates to one client-ready brief.

Meeting Intelligence helps a coordinator gather distributed inputs, identify what is missing or inconsistent, and produce a concise status brief, action register, and targeted follow-up list without manually rebuilding the full picture from every message.

Demonstration boundary: This browser-local scenario uses fictional contributors and deterministic results. The comparison shows the intended operating experience and review design. It does not claim measured client savings, production AI accuracy, or live workplace integration.

The operational payoff

Review the exceptions, not every line of every update.

The workflow does not add a second administrative process. It replaces repeated searching, chasing, reconciling, rewriting, and tracker maintenance with one coordinated review.

Before

The coordinator rebuilds the picture manually.

  • Search email, chat, notes, and trackers for six contributor updates
  • Identify who has not responded and send follow-ups
  • Reconcile conflicting dates and incomplete ownership
  • Rewrite the same facts into a client-facing status
  • Build and maintain a separate action tracker
After

The workflow assembles the package and isolates judgment.

  • Show contributor response status in one view
  • Prepare routine facts from received updates
  • Flag only missing, conflicting, or unassigned items
  • Draft the client brief and action register together
  • Require review of three exceptions before release
