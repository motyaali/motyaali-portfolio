AI Workflow Enablement

The client promise

Bring one recurring administrative process. Leave with a tested workflow that prepares routine work, directs attention to exceptions, preserves human review where judgment matters, and includes the documentation and training required to operate it.

Common starting points

  • Meeting follow-up and coordination
  • Document intake and routing
  • Project status reporting
  • Work-request triage
  • SOP and knowledge retrieval

Four engagement packages

Workflow DiagnosticCurrent-state map, pain and risk analysis, suitability, recommended future state, implementation estimate.
Controlled PilotOne bounded workflow, representative approved data, human-review gates, testing, training, and scale/revise/stop recommendation.
Full Implementation & EnablementApproved production configuration, documentation, permissions, governance, launch support, and administrator handoff.
Optimization & SupportPerformance review, error analysis, regression testing, refresher training, and enhancement backlog.

Good pilot fit

  • One recurring process with a clear owner and measurable delay, rework, or administrative burden
  • Approximately 5 to 20 pilot users
  • One team or business unit
  • Representative, approved data limited to the defined process
  • Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, or another approved workplace platform
  • Willingness to test ambiguity, duplicates, failures, permissions, and adoption

Decision at the end

Scale, revise, or stop based on documented pilot evidence.

What the client receives

  • Workflow assessment and future-state process map
  • Configured workflow in the approved environment
  • Data dictionary and routing matrix where applicable
  • Implementation and end-user guidance
  • Administrator runbook and governance boundary
  • Test plan, evidence log, and acceptance criteria
  • Training package and guided practice
  • Handoff and improvement plan

How risk is controlled

  • Preserve source records and evidence
  • Separate routine preparation from exceptions
  • Require human review for uncertain, sensitive, high-impact, or irreversible outcomes
  • Expose status, errors, and correction history
  • Use representative demo data before sensitive client information
  • Do not promise time savings until baseline and pilot results are measured

Proof you can inspect now

Intelligent Document Intake and Routing: six fictional records, routine-group confirmation, duplicate hold, missing-information handling, explicit classification review, four-routed/two-held controlled outcome, executable browser tests, runbook, and public proof manifest.

Meeting Intelligence: distributed updates assembled into a reviewed brief, action register, exception decisions, and targeted follow-up.

No live client-system integration, production AI accuracy, or measured client savings are claimed.
