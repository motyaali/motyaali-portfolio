Strong pilot signal

  • Recurring work with a clear owner
  • Defined inputs and outputs
  • Measurable delay, manual effort, errors, or rework
  • Representative approved data
  • Accountable people willing to review exceptions

Weak fit

  • No process owner
  • No access to the systems involved
  • One-time creative task
  • Expectation that AI should repair missing source data or undefined policy
  • Autonomous high-impact decision making
