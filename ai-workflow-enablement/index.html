My contribution

Requirements, interactions, tests, and guidance.

I mapped inputs and ownership, separated routine preparation from judgment, built the browser interactions, defined acceptance scenarios, and documented the controls and operating handoff. The public implementation uses fixed synthetic data and local browser logic.

The problem: coordinators reconstruct status from fragmented inputs while missing information and unclear authority hide inside routine work. The design makes those exceptions visible and blocks release until required review is complete.
